# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct · 贡献者公约

## Our Pledge · 我们的承诺

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

我们承诺为所有参与者提供无骚扰的体验，不论年龄、体型、残疾、族裔、性别特征、性别认同与表达、经验水平、教育、社会经济地位、国籍、个人外貌、种族、宗教或性取向。

## Our Standards · 我们的标准

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

有助于营造积极环境的行为示例：

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
-  使用欢迎和包容的语言
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
-  尊重不同的观点和经验
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
-  优雅地接受建设性批评
- Focusing on what is best for the community
-  关注对社区最有利的事情
- Showing empathy towards other community members
-  对其他社区成员表现出同理心

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

不可接受的行为示例：

- The use of sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention or advances
-  使用性化语言或图像、不受欢迎的性关注或挑逗
- Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
-  挑衅、侮辱/贬低性评论，以及人身或政治攻击
- Public or private harassment
-  公开或私下骚扰
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
-  未经明确许可发布他人的私人信息
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting
-  在专业环境中可被合理认为不合适的其他行为

## Enforcement Responsibilities · 执行责任

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any instances of unacceptable behavior.

项目维护者负责澄清和执行可接受行为的标准，并将对任何不可接受的行为采取适当和公平的纠正措施。

## Scope · 适用范围

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces — issues, pull requests, comments, and any other form of communication — and also applies when an individual is representing the project in public spaces.

本公约适用于所有项目空间（issue、PR、评论及其他任何形式的沟通），也适用于个人在公共空间代表项目时。

## Enforcement · 执行

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project team at **lethe@chen.com**. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated and will result in a response that is deemed necessary and appropriate to the circumstances. The project team is obligated to maintain confidentiality with regard to the reporter of an incident.

辱骂、骚扰或其他不可接受的行为可通过 **lethe@chen.com** 向项目团队举报。所有投诉将被审查和调查，并将根据情况作出必要且适当的回应。项目团队有义务对事件举报者保密。
